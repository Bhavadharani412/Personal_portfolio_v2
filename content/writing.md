## Writing Philosophy

Writing is part of my engineering process.

I use technical writing to:

**Build → Investigate → Understand → Explain → Document**

The goal is not simply to publish tutorials. Writing helps me examine why a system works, where its trade-offs are, and what I learned while building it.

## Writing Grounding Rules

- Only documented articles and posts should be represented here.
- Article titles and links must match the documented source.
- Descriptions should reflect the actual subject of the article.
- Do not invent publication dates, readership numbers, awards, or engagement metrics.
- Do not claim expertise based only on having written about a topic.
- Project-related articles should defer to the corresponding project file for detailed technical facts.
- Writing should distinguish between experimentation, learning, implementation, and established experience.
- If an article or link is not documented, do not fabricate it.
